# Product

## Statement

Pocket Ledger is a single-user, offline-first spending tracker for iPhone, installed as a Home Screen web app (an optional native iOS build exists). It records money held in a few places (a safe at home, a pocket, bank accounts), in two currencies, and shows where the money went. It never connects to a bank, never moves money and never uploads data.

## Principles

1. **The ledger is the truth.** Every balance is the sum of its entries and can be rebuilt from them.
2. **Two currencies stay separate.** No total ever combines currencies.
3. **Recording must be fast.** The common case (an expense in the primary currency) takes three taps and a few digits.
4. **Receipts can wait.** A photo can be captured in seconds and turned into an entry later.
5. **Private and offline.** No account, no sync, no analytics. Clear language about where data lives.
6. **Honest states.** Empty, loading and error states say what is going on; nothing is faked.

## Who it is for

One person who manages cash and bank money in two currencies and wants a calm daily habit: record the day's spending in the evening, keep receipts for larger invoices, and see monthly totals by category.

## Core jobs

- Set up currencies and accounts with opening balances.
- Record expenses, income, transfers and currency exchanges.
- Capture receipts and invoices as photos, then add the amount later.
- See balances per account and per currency, today's and this month's spending.
- Browse and search entries by month, type, currency, category and account.
- See monthly reports by category and a six-month trend.
- Reconcile an account to the amount actually counted.
- Track receivables and payables per party: loan given, loan received, repayment received, repayment made, expenses paid by a party on your behalf, settle a balance, and a statement of account per party (debit, credit, Dr/Cr running balance, b/f and c/f) that can be shared as text or CSV.
- Receive a nightly reminder.
- Export entries as CSV; optionally lock the app with biometrics.

## Out of scope (for now)

Budgets, recurring entries, multi-device sync, bank imports, exchange-rate lookups, attachments other than images, more than two currencies in the UI (the data model allows it), encrypted backup files. See BACKLOG.md.
